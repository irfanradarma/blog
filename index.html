About

01

I am an IT Auditor and Data Specialist at the Inspectorate General of Indonesia's Ministry of Finance — a role I've held since 2013, growing from data engineering into AI assurance and governance leadership.

My work sits at the intersection of technology, risk, and public accountability. I built the organization's AI auditing framework from the ground up, synthesizing OECD, NIST AI RMF, and ISO 42001:2023. I have delivered probity audits on nation-wide ERP and core-tax systems, and collaborated with Ernst & Young Indonesia and the World Bank on public-sector audit guidebooks.

I hold a Master of Science from Carnegie Mellon University (Data Analytics), and teach at PKN STAN and the Ministry of Finance Corporate University — translating technical complexity into practical capability for government practitioners.
