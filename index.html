About

Bridging governance,
data, and assurance.

Over a decade at the intersection of technology, risk, and public accountability.

I am an IT Auditor and Data Specialist at the Inspectorate General of Indonesia's Ministry of Finance, where I have spent over a decade shaping how public institutions govern data, manage risk, and adopt technology responsibly.

My work spans the full spectrum: from building the organization's first AI auditing framework — synthesizing OECD, NIST AI RMF, and ISO 42001:2023 — to executing probity audits on nation-wide ERP and core-tax systems.

A graduate of Carnegie Mellon University's Heinz College (M.S. Public Policy & Management – Data Analytics), I bring quantitative rigor to governance questions that typically resist measurement.

Beyond auditing, I teach at PKN STAN and the Ministry of Finance Corporate University, and have advised the Supreme Court, Ministry of Health, and others on continuous monitoring architecture.
